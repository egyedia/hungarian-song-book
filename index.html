Three questions we always get

Is this a ukulele book?

No — it's a songbook for any chord-playing instrument. The ukulele chapter exists so a complete beginner can join in by the end of one camp.

Do I need a guitar?

The chord letters work on guitar, ukulele, piano and accordion — and the back of the book has chord charts for six instruments, including the three-string kontra.

How were the songs chosen?

By ten years of requests: these are the songs Hungarian communities in California actually ask for — one for every occasion of the year, and every one of them copyright-clean.
